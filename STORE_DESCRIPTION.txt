GhostedWatch tells you when your own posts and comments on the .win sites
(patriots.win / scored.co / communities.win) have been removed — including
the kind of removal where you still see your content but everyone else sees a
"removed by community filter" placeholder.

Features:

★ Monitors your own posts and comments and notifies you the moment one is removed
★ Watch anyone's content: a Watch button on every post and comment lets you monitor
  others too — a single item, a whole thread (post + all comments), a user's entire
  history, or a community's new feed — and alerts you when any of it is removed
★ Shows the text/title that was posted, even after it's removed
★ Highlights removed content in-page (threads, user pages and feeds), labeled
  with who/how it was removed
★ Confirms what logged-out visitors actually see (a placeholder, or nothing)
★ Works across the platform's communities and domains
★ Private by design: everything runs in your own browser — no analytics, no tracking,
  no third-party servers, and it never posts or votes on your behalf

Made in America 🇺🇸 · Independent and private by design.
